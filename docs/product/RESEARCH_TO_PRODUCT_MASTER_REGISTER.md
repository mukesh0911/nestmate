# NestMate — Research-to-Product Master Register
**Standard**: COMPREHENSIVE RESEARCH TRACEABILITY MASTER REGISTER  
**Date**: 2026-08-26  

---

## 1. Register Overview

This document establishes the permanent inventory of all 30 research-derived user pain points, unmet market needs, trust/safety requirements, competitive gaps, and algorithmic specifications for the NestMate platform.

---

## 2. Complete Master Register (RPR-001 to RPR-030)

| ID | Domain | Research Finding & User Pain | Priority | Intended Solution | Implementation Components | Expected User Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RPR-001** | Trust & Safety | Fake profiles, unverified strangers, and identity uncertainty cause safety anxiety and fraud risks. | **CRITICAL** | 4-tier progressive trust ladder (Phone OTP → Profile Completeness → Work Email → Govt ID). | `TrustBadge.tsx`, `useAuth.ts`, `trustSignals.ts` | Users see verifiable trust level on every profile card before interacting. |
| **RPR-002** | Trust & Safety | Risk of financial deposit scams and fake property owners. | **CRITICAL** | Government ID upload with admin moderation queue; rule-gated `isVerified` status. | `EditProfile.tsx`, `AdminVerifications.tsx`, `firestore.rules` | High-trust badge granted only upon human admin verification of authentic ID docs. |
| **RPR-003** | Privacy | Mobile numbers and emails posted publicly get harvested by brokers, scammers, and spammers. | **CRITICAL** | Strict PII isolation in `privateUserData/{uid}`; stripped from public collections. | `privateUserData.ts`, `useUserProfile.ts`, `firestore.rules` | Zero contact information is exposed publicly or returned in public browse queries. |
| **RPR-004** | Privacy | Unsafe communication and forced offline contact before establishing mutual trust. | **CRITICAL** | Built-in chat with two-way mutual phone sharing handshake protocol. | `useChat.ts`, `phoneShare.ts`, `ChatPage.tsx` | Phone numbers revealed only when both users click "Share Phone" in active chat. |
| **RPR-005** | Safety | Working women relocating to metro cities face harassment or unsafe flatmate environments. | **CRITICAL** | Female-only filters, verified female flatmate rooms, trust badges, instant reporting. | `listingFilters.ts`, `SeekerDashboard.tsx`, `ExplorePage.tsx` | Women can safely discover verified female-only flatmates and gated accommodations. |
| **RPR-006** | Moderation | Inability to sanction, report, or block abusive actors or scammers on informal platforms. | **CRITICAL** | Non-bypassable report queue (`reports/`), real-time account blocking, and admin actions. | `reportValidation.ts`, `AdminReports.tsx`, `App.tsx` | Harassers are instantly blocked from sessions and flagged to admins for account suspension. |
| **RPR-007** | Trust & Safety | Competitor platforms display fake "verified" icons without real underlying verification. | **HIGH** | Display truth architecture ensuring badges strictly reflect real Auth/Admin verification. | `displayTruth.ts`, `trustSignals.ts`, `TrustBadge.tsx` | Badges reflect authentic verification state only; zero misleading claims. |
| **RPR-008** | Matching | Lifestyle mismatch (sleep, diet, cleanliness, noise, WFH) causes severe flatmate conflict. | **CRITICAL** | 10-question lifestyle questionnaire producing exact mathematical compatibility %. | `SeekerOnboarding.tsx`, `matching.ts`, `useMatches.ts` | Users discover flatmates with compatible daily routines, habits, and lifestyles. |
| **RPR-009** | Matching | Opaque compatibility percentages ("92%") feel arbitrary without transparent reasons. | **MEDIUM** | Real-time match explanation breakdown showing exact shared traits and caveats. | `getMatchReasons` in `matching.ts`, `MatchDetail.tsx` | Users understand exactly why a candidate is compatible (e.g. "Same city, 8 habits match"). |
| **RPR-010** | Matching | Cross-city candidates showing high scores despite being in different geographic regions. | **HIGH** | Mathematical cross-city penalty cap (60% maximum) when cities differ. | `calculateCompatibility` in `matching.ts` | Cross-city candidates are never misrepresented as perfect local matches. |
| **RPR-011** | Matching | Updating lifestyle preferences fails to update compatibility scores across existing feeds. | **HIGH** | Real-time dynamic recalculation on profile snapshot hydration. | `useMatches.ts`, `EditProfile.tsx` | Feeds and candidate cards reflect updated compatibility immediately upon quiz edit. |
| **RPR-012** | Matching | Incomplete profiles or missing quiz answers receiving inflated default match scores. | **HIGH** | String-equality comparison with honest zero score for missing/unshared answers. | `matching.ts` | Incomplete profiles receive 0% match and low-confidence caveats. |
| **RPR-013** | Culture | Western platforms ignore India-specific dietary (veg/non-veg) and cultural preferences. | **HIGH** | Dedicated lifestyle quiz questions covering diet, cooking, and house guest policies. | `SeekerOnboarding.tsx`, `staticData.ts` | Indian urban flatmates filter by vegetarian/non-vegetarian and lifestyle habits. |
| **RPR-014** | Discovery | Disorganized search across chaotic Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats, and broker sites. | **HIGH** | Unified Explore marketplace with separate Rooms and Flatmates tabs. | `ExplorePage.tsx`, `BrowseRooms.tsx`, `BrowseSeekers.tsx` | Single unified destination to browse both available rooms and compatible flatmates. |
| **RPR-015** | Discovery | Misleading geographic claims; listings claiming proximity to tech parks are far away. | **HIGH** | Interactive Leaflet metro map with geocoded cluster pins and locality search. | `MapView.tsx`, `useGeocoding.ts`, `locationResolver.ts` | Visual map-based discovery with accurate geographic positioning across metro cities. |
| **RPR-016** | Listings | Wasted time inquiring on stale, inactive, or already-occupied room listings. | **HIGH** | Listing freshness decay (14/45 days) and owner "Confirm Available" action. | `listingFreshness.ts`, `marketplaceVisibility.ts` | Stale listings automatically decay and get excluded from active discovery feeds. |
| **RPR-017** | Discovery | Viewing unaffordable rooms outside partner rent budget wastes seeker time. | **HIGH** | Exact partner rent range filtering (`budgetMin`/`budgetMax`) and price sorting. | `listingFilters.ts`, `SeekerDashboard.tsx` | Seekers only see rooms within their specified monthly rent capacity. |
| **RPR-018** | Discovery | Lack of granular property filtering (flat type, furnishing, specific amenities). | **HIGH** | Multi-criteria filter pipeline (1BHK/2BHK/3BHK, Furnished/Unfurnished, Wi-Fi, AC, etc.). | `listingFilters.ts`, `ExplorePage.tsx` | Seekers filter inventory by precise physical property requirements. |
| **RPR-019** | Listings | Room owners unable to manage listing status (active, paused, filled) or track views. | **MEDIUM** | Dedicated Owner Dashboard with listing status controls and weekly views analytics. | `OwnerDashboard.tsx`, `useListings.ts`, `EditListing.tsx` | Owners pause/resume listings instantly and monitor applicant interest trends. |
| **RPR-020** | Listings | Owners forgetting to update listing availability leading to ghost inquiries. | **MEDIUM** | 1-tap "Confirm Available" button on Owner Dashboard updating `lastConfirmedAt`. | `OwnerDashboard.tsx`, `useListings.ts` | Active owners keep listings fresh and ranked high with a single tap. |
| **RPR-021** | Owner Triage | Room owners overwhelmed by unordered applicant lists; cannot identify best fit. | **HIGH** | Explainable applicant ranking engine sorting by fit score, budget, trust, and ties. | `applicantRanking.ts`, `OwnerDashboard.tsx` | Owners see ranked applicants with data-backed fit badges and honest caveats. |
| **RPR-022** | Pricing | Competitors paywalling core messaging (Roomster/FlatMate.in pay-to-message). | **HIGH** | Core matching, connection requests, and in-app chat are 100% free with honest pricing. | `PricingPage.tsx`, `useConnections.ts` | Zero paywall barrier to connect and communicate with flatmates. |
| **RPR-023** | Chat | Communication latency and clunky messaging interfaces causing dropped deals. | **HIGH** | Real-time WebSocket chat with typing indicators, read receipts, and photo sharing. | `useChat.ts`, `ChatPage.tsx` | Smooth, modern instant messaging experience between connected users. |
| **RPR-024** | Chat | Message ghosting; users not knowing if the recipient is still active on the platform. | **MEDIUM** | Activity recency indicator ("Active now", "Active 2h ago", "Active 3d ago"). | `getActiveStatus` in `matching.ts`, `SeekerDashboard.tsx` | Users gauge user responsiveness before sending connection requests. |
| **RPR-025** | Notifications | Missing connection requests or incoming messages due to lack of real-time alerts. | **HIGH** | In-app notification center + real-time badge counts + background push support. | `NotificationsPage.tsx`, `public/sw.js`, `useNotifications.ts` | Users are notified immediately of new requests, acceptances, and messages. |
| **RPR-026** | Usability | Accidental skip or dismiss of compatible profile with no way to recover. | **LOW** | 5-second undo toast window on profile skips and saved profiles bookmarks tab. | `SeekerDashboard.tsx`, `useUserProfile.ts` | Users quickly recover accidentally skipped profiles with 1-tap undo. |
| **RPR-027** | Usability | Metro migrants with varying English proficiency needing localized navigation. | **LOW** | 4-language i18n switcher (English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu). | `useTranslation.ts`, `SettingsPage.tsx` | Users navigate core platform actions in their preferred regional language. |
| **RPR-028** | Role System | Users having to create separate accounts to seek a room vs list a spare room. | **HIGH** | Unified account with seamless bidirectional role switching between Seeker and Owner. | `ProfilePage.tsx`, `useUserProfile.ts`, `App.tsx` | Single user account effortlessly toggles between Seeker and Owner modes. |
| **RPR-029** | Session | Route-flickering, data corruption, or onboarding bypasses during page reload. | **HIGH** | Synchronized auth hydration, `OnboardingGuard` state locks, and persistent storage. | `App.tsx`, `useAuth.ts` | Reliable, flicker-free session persistence across browser refreshes. |
| **RPR-030** | Privacy | Lack of self-serve data portability and complete account deletion capabilities. | **HIGH** | GDPR personal data JSON download and full cascade account deletion. | `SettingsPage.tsx`, `useUserProfile.ts` | Users export their full data package or delete their account and data completely. |
